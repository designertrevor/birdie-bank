3. **Drop the ball when the prop carries it.** Megaphone, scorecard, gift box, calendar, link: show the object, not a ball holding it.
4. **Balls are white.** Not lime. Said four times.
5. **Hats get a full outline.** Skin tone stays one colour across a sheet.
6. **Arms off unless they do something.** Arms down in the car, no arms on the hat buddies.
7. **Dots and marks small.** The calendar's pin dot was "too massive" twice.
