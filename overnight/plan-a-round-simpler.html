Get a round going, with or without a date

Planning ahead today asks for a game and a day before anyone else sees a thing. Most real plans start as "who's up for golf soon?" This page lays out what's built, what other apps do, and six calls to make. Pick an option under each one and add a note; your answers save here and I'll read them back.

If you take every recommendation

The new flow

1Plan a round

From Up next. One screen: a message, When / Where / Game and bet all "not yet" by default, who to ask.

2Send

Group text or link. Friends tap I'm in and tick the days that work.

3Sort it out

The plan page shows what's set, what's being voted on and what's open, with the chat under it.

4Lock it in

The organizer locks the day when it's clear. Morning text and roll call work as now.

What goes away

  • Picking a game to get in. The game becomes one row, defaulting to "Group picks".
  • The required day. Set, a few options, or not sure yet.
  • The separate invite and ballot screens. People are on the first screen; the ballot fills from what friends vote and suggest. The organizer can still seed it.
  • Nothing at the tee changes. Roll call, Tee off and the voted game work the same once the day is locked.

Smaller questions for later

  • Should a plan with no day nudge the organizer after a week ("Still want to play? 3 people said they're in")?
  • Course as a vote too, or just set or open?
  • Does a usual (your saved game set-up) fill the Game and bet row as the suggestion, as it does now?
