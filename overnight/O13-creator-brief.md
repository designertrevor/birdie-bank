## The problem

Every golf group has games going: skins, Nassau, Wolf, presses, a side bet on the 18th. Someone keeps it
on the back of a card, the math gets argued in the parking lot, and somebody still owes from three weeks
ago. [App name] keeps score for every game as you play, adds it all up at the end, and keeps a running
tab for the season so nobody has to be the one who remembers.

## The moment to film

The end of the round. Tap to finish and it plays out in one go:

1. **The reveal.** Each game and bet resolves, then the leaderboard races: everyone's total counts up,
   rows pass each other, and the winner climbs to the top and takes the crown.
2. **Settle up.** The fewest payments that clear everything ("Mike pays Dan $14"), each with a one-tap
   button that opens the payee's own app (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle).
3. **The results card.** A picture of the round with the winner celebrating, ready to drop in the group
   chat.

Film your group's reactions. The round itself (a press, the trash talk) is setup; the ending is the
payoff. Your friends join from a link, no download needed.

## How to talk about it

Say it's for **friendly games** with your buddies. The app never holds, sends or collects money; it keeps
score and shows who owes who, and people pay each other in their own apps.

| Say | Avoid |
|---|---|
| friendly games, games with your buddies | gambling, betting app, sportsbook |
| skins, Nassau, the match, side games | odds, lines, bookie, wager money |
| keeps score, settles up, the tab | cash out, winnings, payouts, jackpot |
| play for a few bucks, or for lunch | "make money", "win big" |

You can also play for points or for lunch instead of money; that's in the app too.
