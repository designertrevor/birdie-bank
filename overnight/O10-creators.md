# O10 research (b): golf creators who film money matches and games with friends

Roadmap area 22 (creator list for the S4 creator test, February to April 2027). Research only. Public info only. Checked 2026-10-06.

## Read this first: how solid these numbers are

- **No platform page could be opened.** youtube.com, tiktok.com, instagram.com, Social Blade, GolfScout, Modash, Heepsy, Feedspot and SponsorRadar were all blocked by this session's network proxy. Every count below comes from **third-party trackers as quoted in web search results** (mostly GolfScout snapshots dated 2026-09-22, Modash lists from May to September 2026, and CreatorDB). None was read off the creator's own profile. Treat every number as "reported", and re-check the shortlist by hand before any outreach.
- "Date checked" is the date I saw the number (2026-10-06). The tracker's own snapshot date is in the Source column when the snippet gave one.
- **Median views were not available anywhere.** Where a tracker gave an average, it says "avg". Where I worked out views per video from total views divided by videos, it says "mean (worked out)". Neither one is a median. Unknown is an en dash (–).
- **"unverified"** marks a count with no date, a stale or conflicting count, a handle guessed from a tracker's URL slug, or a content fit I couldn't confirm.
- Links: when the handle was stated in a source, the link is the profile. When it came from a tracker's URL slug, it's marked "(slug)". When I couldn't find the handle, the link is a platform search.
- **Shortfall:** the brief asked for 50 to 100 creators with 10k to 100k followers. I got **50 entries** in the main list. **31** have a reported count inside 10k to 100k (several of those counts are marked unverified). **10** have no count at all. **9** sit just outside the range (from 9.6K up to about 190K, plus one stale count and one cross-platform total) and are listed because their format fits. The 10k to 100k long tail is hard to find through search engines without the platforms themselves. Finishing this list properly is a 1 to 2 hour manual pass on a phone (see "Next steps").

Fit scale: **High** = they regularly film matches or games with friends, often with stakes. **Med** = some matches, or the audience is mostly everyday golfers. **Low** = golf audience but little match content (listed so you can rule them out fast).

## What the research suggests for the creator test

1. **The best fit is a regular cast playing short matches** (Golf Mates Club, Bad Golf, Fore Bros, Divot Dudes). Their audiences already play in groups and care about who won. That's our organizer. Bad Golf (61.6K, about 34K average views) and The Golf Mates Club (27K) are the cleanest in-budget picks, though both are UK, where "money games" might be framed as "a pint on it".
2. **Pros who play creators** (Ruffels, Chance Taylor, Nick Voke, James Nicholas) have strong per-video views and appear in match content, but their viewers watch skill, not group games. Use them for credibility, not as the main channel.
3. **TikTok mid-size accounts have the best views for their follower count** (Mully Golf avg 154K on 58K followers, AndyCastellGolf avg 58K on 16K). But none were confirmed to film money matches. Short "loser pays" or "who owes who" clips could run as paid Spark Ads on their accounts.
4. Gabby Golf Girl's 1v1 money-match format is exactly our story, but she's far above the budget. Use her videos as the reference brief for smaller creators.

## Next steps (manual, about 1 to 2 hours on a phone)
