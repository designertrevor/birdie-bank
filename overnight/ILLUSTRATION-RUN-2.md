## Dark mode (decided)

- Scenes and spots: one file, and the app adds a thin cream outline in dark mode (CSS drop-shadow, 1.5px #fbf7ec on four sides). See `dark-mode-picks.html`.
- Critters: on a colour blob in both themes.
- The sky is cut out wherever it shows through, including gaps between arms and legs. Gaps over grass or another object stay filled.

## Faces (from the picks)

- The banker (pick 11) is the face and demeanor to match: dot eyes, a small simple nose, a calm look.
- Mouths are a tiny short line or nothing. No wide mouth lines.
- Noses are simple.
- Balls are white (cream) with dimples.
- Buddies at small sizes: no mouth, and eyes big enough to see.
