Birdie Bank

Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version. Birdie Bank keeps score and tracks friendly bets for your golf group. We collect only what the app needs to do that. We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we never hold or move money.

This policy explains what Birdie Bank ("we," "us") collects when you use the app at birdie-bank.vercel.app, how we use it, and the choices you have. Birdie Bank is run by Trevor Nielsen. Questions go to design@trevornielsen.com.

What we collect

What you enter in the app.

  • Your name and handicap index, and the names, handicap indexes and optional Venmo usernames of the players you add.
  • Rounds: the course, tees, games, stakes, scores, bets, presses and results.
  • Payments you record between players, and your crews, custom courses and settings.

Your account, if you sign in. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your name and profile picture from Google. We don't receive your Google password or anything else from your Google account.

Suggestions and bug reports. What you write, any photo you attach (like a scorecard or screenshot), an optional email for a reply, and details that help us fix problems: your device type and browser, screen size, and the round you had open.

Technical data. Like any website, our hosting providers receive your IP address and basic request information when the app loads.

Where it's stored

Birdie Bank saves your data on your device first, so it works with no signal. If you sign in, it's also saved to your account on our servers so you can use it on other devices. If you don't sign in, your rounds stay on your device, except for rounds you share live.

Live shared rounds. When you share a round live, its players, course, games, stakes and scores are stored on our servers under a 6-letter code. Anyone with that code or link can view and score that round, so only share it with your group. You can stop sharing at any time.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep score, work out the games and the money, and keep your tab.
  • To sync your data across your devices and to the people in a shared round.
  • To read your suggestions and fix bugs, and to reply if you left an email.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers below, or when the law requires it.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States.
  • Vercel hosts the app.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in, if you use it.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve the app's fonts and icons, so they receive your IP address when the app loads.

Payment links open Venmo or other payment apps directly. Birdie Bank doesn't send them your information, and what you do there is covered by their own privacy policies.

Money

Birdie Bank tracks friendly wagers between friends. It never holds, sends or collects money, and it never asks for bank or card details.

Your choices

  • See and change your data in the app at any time.
  • Back it up to a file from Settings.
  • Delete your account and data by emailing design@trevornielsen.com from the address you signed in with. We'll delete it within 30 days. Data on your device can be erased from Settings.
  • Google access can be removed any time from your Google Account's security settings.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Live shared rounds may be removed after 30 days without activity. Suggestions and bug reports are kept while they're useful for improving the app.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your account data can only be read by you, and access to our servers is limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Age

Birdie Bank is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a minor has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in the app.
